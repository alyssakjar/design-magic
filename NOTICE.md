# Licence and attribution

This is a private, personal collection. Almost none of it is my own work: every
`SKILL.md` carries an `author` field naming who wrote it, and that is the attribution
that matters.

Adding that field is the only change made to any skill — one line in the frontmatter.
Skill bodies, and their `name` and `description`, are untouched from upstream.

The collection was assembled from
[damilareoo/damilares-skills](https://github.com/damilareoo/damilares-skills), curated
by [@damilareoo](https://github.com/damilareoo), who in turn gathered much of it from
other authors.

**That repository carries no licence file**, and neither do the skills that came from
it. Nothing here grants redistribution rights. Its README invites cloning the skills
into your own `~/.claude/skills`, which is what this repository is for — but that is
not permission to republish them. **Do not make this repository public**, and do not
redistribute it, without permission from @damilareoo and from the upstream authors.

Two specifics worth knowing:

- **`frontend-design` is Anthropic's, and points at a licence file it does not have.**
  Its frontmatter reads `license: Complete terms in LICENSE.txt`, but no such file came
  with it. Read the terms at [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills).
- **`12-principles-of-animation` declares `license: MIT`** in its frontmatter. The
  skills from Jakub Krehel and Matt Pocock are MIT in their source repositories.

If any attribution is wrong, the error is mine in copying it, not the author's.
