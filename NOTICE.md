# License and attribution

This is a private collection of skills written by other people. Every `SKILL.md`
carries an `author` field naming who wrote it.

**Many of these skills carry a license of their own, and those licenses differ.** Some
declare one in their frontmatter, some are MIT in the repository they came from, and
some declare nothing at all. The skill itself is where the terms live, so check it
before reusing it anywhere.

**This repository is private and should stay that way.** These licenses are not mine
to re-grant, and nothing here gives anyone permission to redistribute someone else's
work.

Adding the `author` field is the only change made to any skill — one line in the
frontmatter. Bodies, names and descriptions are untouched.

Two specifics worth knowing:

- **`frontend-design` points at a license file it does not have.** Its frontmatter
  reads `license: Complete terms in LICENSE.txt`, but no such file came with it. It is
  Anthropic's; read the terms at
  [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills).
- **`12-principles-of-animation` declares `license: MIT`** in its frontmatter. The
  skills from Jakub Krehel and Matt Pocock are MIT in their source repositories.

If any attribution is wrong, the error is mine in copying it, not the author's.
